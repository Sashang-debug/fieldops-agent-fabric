# KPI Definitions Glossary (FY2025)

Authoritative definitions for FieldOps Finance reporting. All board materials must use these definitions.

## Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)

Contracted recurring subscription revenue annualized at period end. Excludes professional
services, one-time implementation fees, and usage overage. Reported in **USD millions ($M)**.

## Gross Margin

**(Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold) / Revenue**, expressed as a percentage.

Gross Margin does **not** include sales commissions, customer success costs, or R&D.
Commissions are reported separately under Sales & Marketing operating expense.

## Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

Ending ARR from the prior-year cohort divided by starting ARR for that cohort, including
expansion and contraction, excluding new logos. Expressed as a percentage.

## Revenue

GAAP recognized revenue for the period, in **USD millions ($M)**.

## Operating Cash Flow (OCF)

Cash from operations per the statement of cash flows, in **USD millions ($M)**.

# Q2 FY2025 Board Deck — Narrative

Prepared for the quarterly board meeting. Highlights below summarize operating performance.

## Growth

We closed Q2 with strong momentum. **ARR reached $48.2M**, reflecting expansion in the
enterprise segment and a healthier late-stage pipeline. New logos contributed modestly;
most of the lift came from seat expansion in existing accounts.

Revenue for the quarter was **$11.2M**, in line with the internal forecast.

## Profitability

**Gross Margin held at 68% (revenue less COGS and sales commissions)**, which management
views as sustainable at current scale. Sales productivity improved even as we added
capacity in mid-market.

## Retention

Net Revenue Retention finished the quarter at **118%**, consistent with the prior two
quarters and above the 115% plan target.

## Outlook

We expect ARR to continue compounding into H2 as the new packaging rolls out. Detailed
tables are in the appendix; this narrative is intended for discussion, not as a
substitute for the forecast workbook.
